ChatGPT / OpenAI Capabilities

ChatGPT is an OpenAI product and is separate from Abacus.AI. This page summarizes commonly documented categories and may vary by plan, model, and product configuration.

💬

Conversational AI

OpenAI documentation commonly describes ChatGPT-style products as conversational assistants for reasoning and interaction.

🧠

Reasoning

General-purpose reasoning support for explanations, strategy, and clarification across many topics.

✍️

Writing assistance

Drafting and rewriting content, summarization, and communication support for a variety of use cases.

💻

Coding

Code generation, debugging explanations, and assistance with programming tasks as supported by current product capabilities.

📊

Data analysis

Example analysis workflows involving structured data, spreadsheets, and charting support depending on the product and tool set.

🖼️

Image understanding and generation

Availability may vary by model and product; image understanding and generation are commonly discussed categories in OpenAI documentation.

📄

File and document analysis

Document understanding and question answering for uploaded content may be available in specific product experiences and plans.

🔎

Web research / search

Web-connected research features vary by product and may depend on whether the specific tool or subscription includes web access.

🎙️

Voice conversations

Voice-based interaction is a capability category that may be available in selected product experiences and regions.

🔗

Integrations and connectors

Possible integrations, connectors, and workflows may vary by platform, API, and product configuration.

🧩

Custom GPTs / equivalent

Specialized GPT-like experiences may be available in certain product contexts; specifics vary by offering and plan.

⚙️

Productivity workflows

AI-powered assistance for drafting, scheduling, analysis, ideation, collaboration, and iterative task execution.
