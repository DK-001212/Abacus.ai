Understanding AI

AI platforms help people turn models, tools, and workflows into useful systems. This page explains the key concepts in simple language.

What is AI?

Artificial intelligence is the field of building systems that can learn patterns, interpret inputs, and assist with reasoning, prediction, communication, and task completion.

What are AI agents?

AI agents are software systems that can act with some autonomy: they receive a goal, choose steps, use tools, and produce results with a workflow.

What are foundation models?

Foundation models are large pretrained systems that can be adapted for many tasks, including language understanding, generation, reasoning, and multimodal interaction.

What is generative AI?

Generative AI creates new content such as text, images, audio, or structured outputs by learning from patterns in training data.

What is machine learning?

Machine learning is a subset of AI where systems learn from examples and improve their ability to predict, classify, or decide over time.

Why AI platforms are useful?

AI platforms make it easier to access models, connect tools, orchestrate workflows, and bring AI into applications without building everything from scratch.
